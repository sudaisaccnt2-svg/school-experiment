To install the app so it works offline (Home Screen, AR, VR/gyro), this iPad needs the classroom certificate. It's a one-time step:

  1. Tap Download certificate, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install.
  3. Open Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and switch on Asawera Classroom CA.
  4. Come back here and tap I've done it: open the app.
