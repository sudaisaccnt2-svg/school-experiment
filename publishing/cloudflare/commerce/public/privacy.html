Privacy & ad choices

Updated 14 October 2026

Your advertising choice

Checking current advertising settings…

These controls apply to this browser. Choosing “ask me again” does not allow advertising until you accept it on a lesson or home page.

Saved lessons and browser storage

The browser can save website files, 3D models and AR files for offline use. Local storage also remembers your last lesson and advertising choice. Clearing website data in your browser removes those saved files and preferences.

Accounts and school records

Students can create accounts using a username and password without an email address. Usernames identify website-managed accounts; they are not verified email addresses. If an external email sign-in provider is connected, that provider verifies its own identities. The private database stores membership dates, payment plan names, quoted prices, applied discounts and promo-code usage, payment references, access-code hashes, and owner activity records. The website owner can record a student's name, class and a private note. Last seen records online account activity; this website does not currently record lesson-by-lesson progress.

Student authentication providers verify student sign-in when connected. The website owner uses a separate private website password; only its salted verifier is stored in protected server configuration. Owner session tokens are stored as hashes in the private database and expire after eight hours. Signing out revokes that session. Secure, HttpOnly session cookies keep online accounts signed in. Account and payment API responses are not cached with offline lessons. Plain passwords and recovery codes are not stored. Website-managed student accounts store salted password verifiers protected by a server secret, recovery-code hashes, and hashed session tokens in the private database. Student sessions expire after 30 days and signing out revokes that session. Resetting or changing a password revokes the account's old sessions and recovery code. Recovery codes can be copied or downloaded by the student; the website does not save them in browser local storage. Payment-wallet passwords and ad payout bank credentials are not requested by this website.

Deleting your account

You can delete your student account from Account > Delete my account, in the Android app or on this website. The external deletion page is asawera-chemistry-51y.pages.dev/delete-account. Confirm your current password and username. Deletion removes username/password and recovery verifiers, all student sessions, school profile and notes, manual Pro membership and unpaid orders. Downloaded public lessons stay on the device until you clear app or browser storage.

Paid transaction amounts, dates, payment references, redeemed-code history, purchase-token hashes and encrypted Google Play purchase tokens are retained for accounting, subscription verification and preventing fraudulent reuse. Their website username and school profile are removed and replaced with an anonymous deleted-account reference. Account deletion does not cancel Google Play subscriptions; cancel them in Google Play before deleting. Your recovery code lets you regain access if you forgot your password.

Google Play Pro purchases

When Google Play purchases become available, Google processes payment and supplies prices and subscriptions. The server verifies purchase state, product, expiry and an account identifier protected by a server secret before granting Pro, acknowledges purchases and refreshes subscription status. Raw payment card details are never collected by this website or app. Google Play tokens are encrypted in the private database; Google push notifications are authenticated. See Google Payments privacy.

Advertising providers

When enabled and allowed by a free visitor, Google AdSense, Google Ad Manager or the connected Prebid providers serve advertisements. Google uses its own auctions. The Prebid option supports PubMatic, Magnite, Index Exchange, OpenX, Sovrn and Microsoft/Xandr connections, with the highest eligible net USD bid winning. Each provider needs its own account and inventory approval. Ad services may collect or receive information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, page URLs, cookies, identifiers and ad interaction data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Third parties may set or read cookies and use web beacons. Requesting non-personalized ads does not mean that no cookies or device data are used.

This version requests non-personalized Google advertising and sends its configured child or teen treatment. Prebid requests conservatively use child restrictions for school inventory, disable device storage and user syncing, and require provider approval for this audience. This does not prevent an ad creative from making third-party requests or using storage according to provider policy. Child-directed requests are excluded from Open Bidding partners by Google. It only requests ads for visits Cloudflare identifies as Pakistan. Pro, offline visits, visitors who decline, and visits with an unavailable membership check load no ad SDK. The Prebid library is excluded from offline lesson downloads. Adding a provider requires a new advertising choice from the visitor.

The site does not send account email addresses, private student notes or payment references as ad targeting fields. Hosting and advertising providers may process ordinary request information according to their own policies.

Android app

The Asawera Chemistry Android app bundles lessons, practice games and AR scene models on your phone. The camera is used for native AR, and the app renders those scenes locally. AR on supported devices uses Google Play Services for AR. Online account checks and secure session cookies connect the app to the same Pro membership and owner controls as this website.

When app advertising is approved and enabled, Google AdMob serves home banners and occasional ads at lesson breaks. Its SDK can process IP addresses, device and app information and ad interaction data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalized advertising with the configured school audience restrictions. Pro accounts, owner accounts, offline sessions and unavailable membership checks receive no app advertising. Google privacy choices appear in the app when required. Payment profile information and payouts are managed directly in Google’s private dashboard.

Questions or account data requests

Contact the website owner through your class or school. A public support email will appear here when configured.

Ad payout bank information and payment-provider account settings are managed directly with those providers. Browser ad choices do not delete cookies already set by an advertising provider; use your browser's website-data controls for that.
