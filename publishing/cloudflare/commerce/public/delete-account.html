Delete without signing in

If your account is suspended or you cannot open its account screen, you can still delete it with your username and current password here.

What deletion removes

Your username, sign-in and recovery verifiers, all account sessions, school profile and private notes, Pro access, and unpaid orders are removed. Public lessons already downloaded stay on your phone until you clear app or browser storage.

Paid records and subscriptions

Paid transaction records and purchase tokens remain without your website username or school profile for accounting, subscription verification and fraud checks. Google Play subscriptions are separate: cancel them in Google Play before deleting your account.

Manage Google Play subscriptions

If you just completed deletion, your old password, recovery code and sessions will no longer work.
